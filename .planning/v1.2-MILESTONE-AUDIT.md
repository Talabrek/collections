## E2E Flows

| Flow | Steps Verified | Status |

### Flow 1: Full Collection Add
1. Player picks up collectible item
2. Player right-clicks collectible (ItemUseListener triggers)
3. AddPreviewGUI opens showing collection grid
4. Player sees current progress and "after adding" preview
5. Player clicks Yes button
6. Item added to journal (playerDataManager.addItem())
7. Item consumed from hand
8. Progress notification sent
9. Milestone notification sent if threshold crossed
10. GUI transitions to CollectionDetailGUI with highlight

### Flow 2: Radar Lifecycle
1. Player equips collector helmet
2. ArmorChangeListener detects HEAD slot change
3. GoggleManager returns tier based on helmet
4. RadarManager.showRadar() creates boss bar
5. RadarTask runs periodically, updates radar
6. Player walks around, radar updates count and direction
7. Player removes helmet
8. RadarManager.hideRadar() removes boss bar

### Flow 3: Tier Visibility with Radar
1. EPIC/LEGENDARY collectible spawns
2. Player without goggles cannot see it (visibility check blocks)
3. Radar does not detect it (canPlayerSeeCollectible filters)
4. Player equips master goggles (RARE tier)
5. Collectible becomes visible
6. Radar now detects and displays it
